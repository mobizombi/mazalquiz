What is different

  • Templates per event: wedding, bridal shower, birthday, anniversary, bar/bat mitzvah.
  • Wrong answers are pre-written - you only fill in the right one.
  • Built-in "who is more likely" questions with the couple's names.
  • Guests do not sign up or install anything.
  • Full Hebrew (right-to-left) support as well as English.

When Kahoot is the better fit

If you are a teacher building question banks for lessons with per-student reports, a classroom tool fits better. MazalQuiz is built for one big moment at a party.
