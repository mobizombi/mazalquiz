When to run it

During cocktail hour while guests mingle, between courses to bring the energy back, or at the rehearsal dinner for a more intimate round.

Tips

  • 10-15 questions is the sweet spot.
  • Mix in "who is more likely" questions - they get the biggest laughs.
  • Let the DJ or MC run the game.
  • Check there is signal or Wi-Fi at the venue.
  • Announce a small prize for the winner.

FAQ

How do guests join?

A QR code and a 5-digit code appear on screen. Guests scan with their camera, pick a nickname, and they are in. No app, no sign-up.
