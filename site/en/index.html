Why a live quiz works at parties

Everyone plays. Grandma at the back table and the college friends alike - anyone with a phone is in within seconds.

It breaks the ice. A quiz about the guests of honor sparks laughs, debates ("what do you mean SHE is always late?!") and stories nobody has heard.

It only takes 10-15 minutes - enough to lift the room between courses or before the dancing, without stealing the show.

FAQ

How much does it cost?

MazalQuiz is free to use. Create a quiz and play with your guests without paying or signing up.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan a QR code on the screen or open the site and type a 5-digit code. Any phone with a browser works.

What do we need at the venue?

A screen or projector connected to a laptop (or a smart TV with a browser), and mobile data or Wi-Fi for guests. Whoever runs the game clicks "Next" between questions - the DJ, the MC or a friend.

Can we write our own questions?

Yes. Edit any template question, switch questions off, and add as many of your own as you like.

How is the winner decided?

Correct answers earn points, and faster answers earn more. A leaderboard appears after each question and a podium crowns the top three at the end.
